THE CHAKRAVYUHA

The Clockwork Labyrinth of Iron and Death

01

THE FORMATION EMERGES

On the thirteenth day of the Kurukshetra War, the crimson soil of the battlefield groans under the weight of an unprecedented terror: the Chakravyuha, a vast, spinning clockwork maze of soldiers, war elephants, and impenetrable iron formations designed by Guru Drona to systematically crush the Pandava army.

02

ARJUNA IS ABSENT

With Arjuna lured far away to the southern front by the Samsaptaka oath-bound warriors, the ancient trap moves forward unchallenged, grinding through Pandava divisions and threatening to bring an absolute end to the war before dusk.

03

ABHIMANYU IS SUMMONED

Desperate to halt its murderous advance, King Yudhisthira turns to Arjuna's sixteen-year-old son, Abhimanyu—the only warrior present in the entire army who possesses the sacred knowledge to pierce the revolving outer rings of Drona's formation.

04

THE DANGEROUS PROMISE

Though Abhimanyu warns with solemn honesty that he knows only how to enter the deadly labyrinth and not how to break back out, Yudhisthira vows upon his honor to follow close behind his golden chariot to keep the breach open.

THE SEVENTH CIRCLE

The Breach Closed. The Final Stand of the Isolated Prince.

01

THE CHARIOT SURGES

Abhimanyu's golden chariot surges forward, his bow tearing through six concentric rings of the Kaurava line with unyielding ferocity.

02

THE ENTRANCE IS SEALED

But a sudden, deafening crash echoes behind him as King Jayadratha seals the entrance, severing the Pandava army from the breach and trapping young Abhimanyu completely alone within the deadly seventh circle.

03

THE CODE DESTROYED

The rules of honorable war vanish in an instant as seven legendary commanders—Karna, Drona, and Duryodhana among them—encircle the isolated prince.

04

THE CHARIOT WHEEL

His bow is shattered, his horses are slain, and he is forced to make a desperate stand with only a chariot wheel as a shield.

THE RULES OF THE CHAKRAVYUHA

Ancient Formations Translated into Labyrinth Mechanics

RULE I

ESCAPE THE FORMATION

The player must guide the ball out of the maze using the keyboard arrow keys.

RULE II

AVOID THE MOVING PADDLES

The moving rectangular obstacles represent the shifting formations of the Chakravyuha. If the ball touches any moving paddle, it is immediately returned to the center.

RULE III

UNLIMITED ATTEMPTS

There is no limit to the number of attempts. Every collision returns the ball to the center, allowing the player to try again.
